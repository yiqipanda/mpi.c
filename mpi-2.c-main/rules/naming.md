# Naming Rules

Follow the project's naming conventions consistently.

## General Guidelines
- **Classes/Interfaces:** `PascalCase`
- **Methods/Functions:** `camelCase`
- **Variables/Properties:** `camelCase`
- **Constants:** `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE`
- **Packages/Namespaces/Folders:** `lowercase` or `kebab-case`
- **REST APIs:** Use plural resource names (e.g., `/users`, `/orders`).

## Principles
- Names should clearly express intent and purpose.
- Avoid abbreviations unless they are industry standard (e.g., `id`, `html`, `db`).
- Boolean variables should often start with a verb like `is`, `has`, or `should`.
